Nepal

Here are some pictures from my trip to Nepal! I was there from June to August 2025, and mostly stayed in the Boudha area while I was studying at RYI. I also travelled a bit around Kathmandu, and at the end of my time there I took a trip to Pokhara and Lower Mustang, including Jomsom, Kagbeni, and Muktinath.
