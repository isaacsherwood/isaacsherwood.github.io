Lower Mustang

At the end of my trip I was fortunate enought to visit Lower Mustang. I drove from Kathmandu to Pokhara, and then up North to Jomsom, then through Kagbeni to Muktinath. It was an absolutely beautiful trip, and I got to visit some of the oldest towns and monasteries in Nepal.
