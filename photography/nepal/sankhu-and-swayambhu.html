Sankhu

Sankhu is a beautiful little town about an hour from Kathmandu. It is known for the sacred Vajrayogini temple, a cave where Milarepa is said to have meditated, and a monument to Nagarjuna. I got to visit the temple, hike to Milarepa cave, and attend a teaching at the Zen center nearby.
